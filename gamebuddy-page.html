02 / Closed-loop behaviorExplore one documented System0 rule

When does
the agent act?

The body continues executing while a decision is in flight. A new preference is applied on the next physics tick, while an already committed attack, roll, or bow draw follows its native lifecycle.

Try changing intent, stamina, and enemy distance. This simplified example assumes a usable bow and arrows, healthy HP, and Kiting. It examines the gate for a new draw, not a physics simulation.

This is one descriptive test, not a success-rate estimate or a latency guarantee. The recorded combat implementation uses remote System1 requests capped at 1 Hz; it does not validate the local 5 Hz design above.

Questions behind
the project.

  • Understanding intentionsHow can a companion understand what a player wants beyond explicit chat prompts?
  • Sharing controlHow should a player guide, constrain, or interrupt an agent without breaking the flow of play?
  • Participating in playWhat makes an AI presence feel like a meaningful companion in an interactive world?
  • Social interaction & creativityHow might virtual companions communicate social cues and contribute to collaborative play and creativity?

These are exploratory questions, rather than completed research findings.
